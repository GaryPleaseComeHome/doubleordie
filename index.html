The rules

16 games. One double. Everything to play for.

  1. Pick the spread. Choose one team in every NFL game against the listed line.
  2. Rank your confidence. Use each value from 1–16 exactly once.
  3. Double one. Your 2X game doubles the points for a win and the penalty for a loss.
  4. Make every point count. Wrong picks subtract the assigned value. The maximum weekly score is 152.

Saving locks your card. Review all picks before confirming; contact the commissioner if a correction is needed afterward.
